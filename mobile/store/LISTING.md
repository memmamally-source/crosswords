## Full description

> A crossword for every obsession.
>
> Crossword Hub is a vintage-newspaper crossword app with a new puzzle every day, a quick daily Mini, a weekly Brutal Challenge and themed crosswords about the things you love, from TV, film, sport and gaming to science, history and philosophy.
>
> EVERY DAY
> • The Daily: a full crossword that gets harder from Monday to Saturday, with the biggest grid on Sunday.
> • The Mini: six quick clues, perfect for a coffee break.
> • The Brutal Challenge: one fiendish puzzle a week. Can you finish it?
> • Keep your streak going and watch your statistics grow.
>
> PLAY WITH FRIENDS
> • Crossword Duels: race a friend to finish the same crossword, live. Send a link, or find an opponent with Quick Match.
> • Help me solve: invite friends to fill in a crossword with you, together.
>
> WORD GAMES
> • Word Ladder: change one letter at a time to climb from one word to another. A new Daily Ladder every day, and unlimited practice.
> • Clue Blitz: how many clues can you crack in 60 seconds?
> • Survival: three lives, and the clues get harder.
>
> HINTS YOUR WAY
> • Reveal a letter or a word when you're stuck, or solve with no hints to earn more.
> • Create a free account for 5 hint credits every day, and keep your progress on all your devices.
>
> MADE FOR SOLVING
> • Type with your phone's keyboard, or use Crossword Hub's own letter keys.
> • Pencil mode, check and reveal, auto-check, and a dark theme.
> • Works offline. No account needed. No ads.

## Google Play: Data safety

Answer **Yes**, the app collects user data, and **No**, it doesn't share any with third parties. All data is **encrypted in transit**. Users **can request deletion**: in the app (Account → Delete account) and by email.

| Data type | Collected? | Optional? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → Email address | Yes | Yes (only with an account) | Account management |
| Personal info → User IDs | Yes | Yes (accounts, and guests in duels) | App functionality, account management |
| App activity → Other user-generated content (duel display names) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| App activity → Other actions (puzzles solved, times, streaks, credits, duel results) | Yes | Yes | App functionality |
| Location, contacts, photos, files, payment info, health, messages, audio | No | | |
| App info and performance (crash logs, diagnostics) | No | | |
| Advertising ID | No | | |

**Ads:** No. **App access:** all features are usable without signing in. Accounts are optional (email and password).

## Google Play: Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

- Category: Game (puzzle/word).
- Violence, fear, sexuality, language, drugs, gambling: **No** to all.
- Do users interact or exchange content? **Yes.** Players in a duel see each other's chosen display name and progress. There's no chat. Names are filtered for offensive words, and the duel lobby and result screens have **Report name** and **Block player** buttons.
- Shares the user's location: No. Digital purchases: No.

Expected result: Everyone / PEGI 3 (possibly with "Users Interact").

## Google Play: Target audience

13 and over (choose the 13–15, 16–17 and 18+ groups). It isn't designed for children, so don't enter the Families programme.

## Apple: App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Data **linked to the user**, used **only for App Functionality**, **not used for tracking**:

- Contact Info → Email Address
- Identifiers → User ID

- User Content → Other User Content (duel display names)

No Usage Data, Diagnostics, Location or Purchases. Tracking: **No**.
